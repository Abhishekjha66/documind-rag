ACME Corp Employee Leave Policy (Sample)

Annual leave: Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

Sick leave: Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave: Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave can be taken in the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

Work from home: Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head.

Notice period: Leave requests of more than 5 days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.
